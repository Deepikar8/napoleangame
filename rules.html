Empire & Coalition

Rules of Engagement — A Campaign of Crowns and Coin
▶ Play the Game

Overview

Empire & Coalition is a Monopoly-inspired board game for 2–5 players set during the Napoleonic Wars. Each player commands a historical figure racing around a 40-space map of Europe, buying territories, building armies, and crushing rivals into bankruptcy.

The game ends either by Strategic Victory (seize Paris and two Battle Territories) or at the end of Round 20, when the richest Commander wins by net worth.

Starting treasury: every Commander begins with 1,500₣ at Mobilization (space 0).

Turn Structure

  1. Roll the Dice — two six-sided dice. Doubles grant an extra roll; three consecutive doubles sends you straight to Exile.
  2. Move — advance that many spaces around the board.
  3. Resolve the space — buy, pay rent, draw a card, or follow the corner rule.
  4. Build — optionally spend money to place Regiments or upgrade to an Army Corps on territories you own outright.
  5. End Turn — pass to the next Commander.
Passing Mobilization (space 0) mid-move collects +200₣ from the bank at the correct step.

Building Regiments & Army Corps

  • Requires owning the full color group.
  • Costs the group's build cost per level.
  • Even-build rule: no territory in a group may have more buildings than any other in the same group — you must build evenly across the group.
  • Maximum 5 levels: levels 1–4 are Regiments, level 5 is an Army Corps.

Purchasing & Auction

Landing on an unowned property offers it to you at the listed price. If you decline, an open auction is held immediately for all other active players:

  • Minimum bid is ½ the listed price.
  • Each bidder may bid at preset amounts (½, ¾, full price, or current high +10₣) or pass.
  • The highest bidder acquires the property at their bid price.
  • If all players pass, the property remains unowned.

Players in Collapse State (≤ 0₣) cannot purchase.

Exile on Elba

A player is exiled by landing on Exiled to Elba! (space 30), drawing the Exile Diplomacy card, or rolling three consecutive doubles.

Each exile turn, choose one of:

  1. Roll for Doubles — escape and move that many spaces. After 3 failed attempts the player pays 50₣ and is released automatically.
  2. Pay 50₣ — immediate release; then roll and move normally.
  3. Use a "Get Out of Exile" card — free escape; then roll and move normally.

Elimination

When a player cannot cover a debt, their assets are force-liquidated: buildings sell first, then cheapest properties, to raise funds. If their treasury reaches zero with no assets remaining, they are eliminated — properties return to the bank and their turns are skipped.

If only one Commander remains after eliminations, they win immediately.

Victory Conditions

Strategic Victory (immediate)

Own Paris (space 39) and at least 2 of the 3 Battle Territories (Austerlitz, Jena-Auerstedt, Wagram).

Net-Worth Victory (Round 20)

If no Strategic Victory occurs by the end of Round 20, the player with the highest net worth (cash + property prices + building costs) wins.

Last Commander Standing

If all rivals are eliminated, the surviving Commander wins immediately.
